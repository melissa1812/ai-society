About us

Who we are and why we exist

Who we are

The University of Birmingham AI Society is a student-run community for anyone curious about artificial intelligence, whatever you study and however much you already know.

Our vision

A campus where AI is built and used on purpose: ethically, responsibly and for the benefit of people.

Our aspirations

Hands-on workshops, guest speakers, a podcast and a place to debate the hard questions, so every member leaves able to do more with AI and think harder about it.

How and why we started

AI was changing every subject on campus faster than the conversation about it. We wanted a place where students could learn the tools, ask the uncomfortable questions and build things together.

Our tagline is the standard we hold ourselves to. Power without responsibility is not innovation, so every session asks what a solution can do and what it should do.
